Marissa Caitlyn Ladatto

Marissa Caitlyn Ladatto was born on December 17, 2001, in Duncanville, Texas. She was an only child who loved her family and friends deeply.

She graduated from Bishop Dunne Catholic School, where she was voted “Most Likely to be Famous” and “Most Talkative.” She attended Dallas College pursuing a teaching degree in history and volunteered with her church to help the homeless.

Her poems are preserved in the collection Half Saint, Half Scar.

Marissa died on December 30, 2022, at the age of 21 from accidental fentanyl poisoning after taking a counterfeit pill she knew contained fentanyl but did not know would be lethal.

As her mother, I carry that truth with me every single day.

Marissa knew the risk. She did not know one pill would end her life.

In her own handwriting, Marissa copied these words from Lamentations:

Because of the Lord’s great Love, we are not consumed, for his compassions never fail. They are new every morning; great is your faithfulness. I say to myself, “The Lord is my portion; therefore I will wait for him.”
— Lamentations 3:22-24
