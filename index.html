Our Mission

Prevention begins before risk becomes tragedy.

Marissa’s Voice Inc. is a survivor-led nonprofit dedicated to reducing the harm of illicit fentanyl and unnecessary opioid exposure. We provide education, free naloxone resources, and advocate for non-opioid pain treatment options so other families do not have to endure the same loss.
