Half Saint, Half Scar

The Handwritten Poems of Marissa Caitlyn Ladatto

Half Saint, Half Scar is a collection of Marissa Caitlyn Ladatto’s handwritten poems, preserved exactly as she wrote them.

A collection born of light and ache, from a girl who painted storms and named them poems.

Her work is raw, unfiltered, and deeply personal. The poems explore grief, addiction, love, trauma, and resilience — capturing the fragile space between pain and healing.

From the poem “Elastic”

Why do I bend myself
To suit
Every Single One Of Your Needs?
I’m Glass,
Not Elastic
